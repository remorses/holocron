Use a hyphen (`-`) instead of an em dash (`—`) to join the page title and site name in the `<title>` tag.

The em dash is the widest separator character, so it wastes pixel budget before Google truncates the title in search results, and there is almost no SEO data on it because nobody uses it. The hyphen is narrower, reads naturally as `Page Title - Site Name`, and is the separator Google rewrites the least. The same change applies to the 404 page title.
